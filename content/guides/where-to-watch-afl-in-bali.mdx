# Where to Watch AFL in Bali (2026 Guide)

Bali and AFL go together better than you'd expect. The island's enormous Australian tourist and expat population means that venues across Seminyak, Canggu, and Kuta have been showing Aussie rules football for years — and they've got it down to a science.

This is your complete guide to watching AFL live in Bali: the best areas, when games kick off local time, and how to find a bar that actually has the rights to show it.

## What Time Does AFL Kick Off in Bali?

Bali is UTC+8 (WITA). During the AFL season (March–September), most of eastern Australia is on AEST (UTC+10), putting Bali two hours behind.

| AFL Kick-Off (AEST) | Bali Time (WITA) |
|---|---|
| 1:45 PM Saturday | 11:45 AM |
| 4:35 PM Saturday | 2:35 PM |
| 7:25 PM Saturday | 5:25 PM |
| 12:10 PM Sunday | 10:10 AM |
| 3:20 PM Sunday | 1:20 PM |

Saturday afternoon AFL is comfortably watchable in Bali — you'll catch the games from late morning through early evening, leaving plenty of time for dinner and a sunset session after.

## The AFL Season Calendar

- **Pre-season:** February
- **AFL Premiership Season:** March – late September
- **Finals:** September
- **AFL Grand Final:** Last Saturday in September (one of the biggest days on the Bali sports bar calendar)

## Best Areas for AFL in Bali

### Seminyak (Best Overall)

Seminyak is the AFL heartland of Bali. The streets around Jalan Kayu Aya, Jalan Petitenget, and Seminyak Square have the highest density of sports bars with reliable AFL coverage. You'll often see handwritten signs on chalkboards outside advertising the weekend's AFL schedule.

### Canggu (Best for Vibe)

Canggu draws a younger, digital nomad crowd. The sports bars here tend to be more relaxed — good for a longer session watching multiple games in a day.

### Kuta & Legian (Most Accessible)

Tourist-friendly and easy to navigate on foot. Poppies Lane 1 and 2 have several Aussie-oriented bars that reliably show AFL.

### Ubud (Limited Options)

You can find AFL in Ubud, but you'll need to ask around. A handful of expat-oriented venues show it, but it's not the same as the coast.

## AFL Grand Final in Bali

The AFL Grand Final (last Saturday in September) is one of the biggest days on the Bali social calendar. Bars across the island go all out — expect reserved seating, themed drinks, and full rooms of Aussies in team colours. Book your spot at least 2-3 days in advance.

## Tips for Watching AFL in Bali

- **Not every bar has the rights.** A minority of Bali's bars have paid for the streaming package giving them legal access to AFL. If a bar seems uncertain, move on.
- **The morning games are easy.** Bali's timing means most AFL kicks off in the morning to early afternoon — ideal for a session that ends in time for a beach sunset.
- **Ask at your hotel.** Most can point you to the nearest sports bar showing Aussie sport.
- **For big games, book ahead.** Grand Final and finals series games fill up quickly.
