Seoul is a late-night city that takes its sport seriously, and for expats and visiting sports fans, Itaewon is where most of the action happens. The district has long been the international hub of Seoul — driven partly by proximity to the US military base at Yongsan — and the result is a cluster of sports bars that cover an unusually wide range of leagues, including plenty of American sport that you rarely see broadcast properly elsewhere in Asia.

EPL and Champions League are popular with the British and European expat crowd. NFL and NBA draw the American community, which is large and vocal here. And K-League — South Korea's domestic football competition — has a genuine local following that gives Seoul sports bars a dimension most other Asian expat bar scenes lack.

The timezone sits at UTC+9. A Saturday 3 p.m. EPL kickoff hits at midnight Seoul time — tough but manageable for a city where bars are still full at 3 a.m. The bigger challenge is the NFL: Sunday afternoon games in the US start at 2 a.m. Monday morning Seoul time, with Sunday Night Football kicking off around 9 a.m. Monday. Seoul's military community means there is actual demand for these slots, so bars serve them.

## Best Overall — Wolfhound Irish Pub (Itaewon)

[Wolfhound](/venues/wolfhound-itaewon-seoul) on Itaewon-ro has been the cornerstone of expat sport watching in Seoul for years. It is an Irish pub done properly — Guinness on tap, wooden bar, and a crowd that actually wants to watch the football rather than just be seen in a bar showing football. The screen setup is solid and coverage is comprehensive.

**What makes it special:**

- Reliable satellite coverage — EPL, Champions League, Six Nations, Heineken Champions Cup
- Irish expat regulars who make Six Nations weeks feel like genuine international fixtures
- Full Irish menu: proper breakfasts, pies, and a Sunday roast that draws a crowd on its own
- Central Itaewon location — easy Subway or Kakao T ride from most parts of Seoul
- Open late enough to cover the full EPL Saturday slate, including the midnight kickoff

**Best for:** Premier League, Six Nations, Champions League, Heineken Champions Cup

**Price range:** Mid-range ($$). Pints from ₩9,000.

**Rating:** 4.6 / 5

## Best for American Sport — Ol' 55 Sports Bar (Itaewon)

[Ol' 55](/venues/ol-55-sports-bar-seoul) is the bar that the American expat and military community gravitates toward when the NFL season starts. It opens for early-morning NFL kickoffs without making it feel like a punishment — there is a proper breakfast menu, the screens are well-positioned, and the crowd that shows up at 2 a.m. for a Monday Night Football opener knows they have made a choice and is fine with it.

**What makes it special:**

- Best NFL coverage in Seoul — all the major games, including Red Zone access during the season
- Opens specifically for early-morning US sports timeslots — Super Bowl, NBA Finals, World Series
- American bar food done well: wings, burgers, nachos at the right price
- NBA gets regular treatment — Curry and LeBron fans have a home here
- NASCAR and college football also covered — the military community creates demand for sports most Asian bars ignore

**Best for:** NFL, NBA, MLB, NASCAR, college football

**Price range:** Mid-range ($$). Beers from ₩8,000.

**Rating:** 4.5 / 5

## Best Craft Beer and Sport — Craftworks Taproom (Itaewon)

[Craftworks Taproom](/venues/craftworks-taproom-itaewon) proved that craft beer and sport do not have to be mutually exclusive. The bar pioneered Seoul's craft beer movement and has built a loyal following that comes for the Korean-brewed IPAs and stays for the EPL. The atmosphere is a notch more relaxed than the pure sports bars, which suits certain fixtures and certain moods.

**What makes it special:**

- Best craft beer selection in Itaewon — Korean-brewed IPAs, stouts, and seasonal beers alongside imports
- EPL and Champions League shown on multiple screens without the bar becoming overwhelmingly sport-focused
- Strong food menu — Korean-inflected bar food that goes beyond the standard burger
- Popular with a younger, more mixed crowd of Koreans and expats
- The rooftop terrace is one of the best spots in Itaewon for an evening pint before a late match

**Best for:** EPL, Champions League, casual sport watching with good beer

**Price range:** Mid-range ($$). Craft pints from ₩10,000.

**Rating:** 4.5 / 5

## Best for K-League and Local Atmosphere — Stadium or Supporter Bars

For K-League, the experience worth having is the stadium — [Seoul World Cup Stadium](https://www.fcseoul.com) (FC Seoul) and [Jamsil Stadium](https://www.seoulelandfc.co.kr) (Seoul E-Land) are both accessible and the supporter culture is worth experiencing firsthand. Korean football fans are organised, enthusiastic, and welcoming to visiting fans who make the effort.

For bars with a K-League following, look for venues near the stadiums on match days. The bars around the World Cup Stadium area in Mapo fill up with FC Seoul supporters before and after home games — a genuinely local sporting experience that Itaewon's expat bars cannot replicate.

**What makes it special:**

- K-League supporter culture is organised and passionate — chants, banners, choreography
- Local bars near stadiums give you actual Korean sports bar culture rather than expat approximations
- K-League season runs March to November — plenty of opportunities
- FC Seoul vs. Jeonbuk Motors (the classic rivalry) is the fixture to plan around
- Combining Korean BBQ with a match is an experience unique to Seoul — find a venue near the stadium that does both

**Best for:** K-League, AFC Champions League, Korean national team fixtures

**Price range:** Budget ($). Beers from ₩5,000 at local bars.

**Rating:** 4.4 / 5

## Best Late-Night Option — Homo Hill and Itaewon Side Streets

Itaewon runs late — the main strip and its side streets stay busy until the early hours. When the midnight EPL kickoff stretches to 2 a.m. and you need somewhere to debrief, the bars immediately surrounding [Wolfhound](/venues/wolfhound-itaewon-seoul) and Ol' 55 are still open. This is less about a single venue and more about knowing that Itaewon does not close when the sport finishes.

**What makes it special:**

- Itaewon licensing allows later trading than most Korean bar districts
- Several bars on the main strip have screens even if sport is not the primary offering
- Post-match atmosphere on a good EPL night can be excellent — particularly after a derby result
- Kakao T (Korea's Uber equivalent) is reliable from Itaewon at any hour

**Best for:** Post-match, late Champions League nights, unwinding after NFL

**Price range:** Mid-range ($$). Drinks vary.

**Rating:** 4.2 / 5

## Best Upscale Option — Grand Hyatt Seoul Sports Bar (Itaewon-adjacent)

The [Grand Hyatt Seoul](/venues/grand-hyatt-seoul-bar) sits on the hill above Itaewon and has a well-equipped bar that shows major sport on reliable feeds. It is significantly more expensive than the strip below, but offers a polished environment and an AV setup that hotel venues in this tier tend to do well.

**What makes it special:**

- Premium AV setup with high-quality screens — no resolution or feed quality issues
- Full cocktail and spirits menu alongside standard beer
- Comfortable seating — worth considering for a long Champions League night
- Hotel location means you can walk upstairs when the match is over
- Business crowd during the week transitions to sport-focused on weekends

**Best for:** Champions League finals, Super Bowl, major one-off events

**Price range:** Premium ($$$). Pints from ₩15,000.

**Rating:** 4.2 / 5

### Getting to Itaewon

Itaewon Station on Line 6 is a two-minute walk from the main strip. Last metro runs around 1 a.m. After that, Kakao T is the default — download the app before you need it, and set the language to English in settings. Fares are reasonable by Western standards.

### The Midnight EPL Problem

The EPL Saturday 3 p.m. kickoff lands at midnight Seoul time — a normal night out in Itaewon, but worth pacing your evening if you want to be coherent at kick off. Wolfhound is the reliable bet. Arrive by 11 p.m. for the late kickoff to guarantee a seat.

### Korean BBQ and Sport

Some bars in the Itaewon area combine table grills with screens — this sounds gimmicky and it is not. Eating Korean BBQ at a table with a screen showing the match is a distinctly Seoul experience worth seeking out at least once. Ask locally or check recent reviews for current venues doing this well.

### What to Budget

Seoul is mid-range by Asian expat city standards — cheaper than Hong Kong or Singapore, broadly comparable to Bangkok. Expect to pay ₩8,000–10,000 for a draught pint at an Itaewon sports bar, ₩12,000–15,000 at hotel bars. Korean convenience stores (GS25, CU, 7-Eleven) sell cold beer cheaply if you need to pre-game on a budget.

### Hongdae and Gangnam

Itaewon is the clear choice for sport, but Hongdae (university district, younger crowd) has bars with screens and a livelier weekend atmosphere for EPL. Gangnam has upscale cocktail bars that sometimes show sport but it is not their focus — not worth the trip specifically for a match.
