Dubai is a global city built on ambition, and its sports bar scene reflects that. Whether you want to watch the Premier League in a 5-star hotel lounge, catch the cricket with fellow South Asian expats, or stay up late for the NFL, there is a venue for you. The timezone (GST, UTC+4) puts European football in prime evening slots and makes UFC main cards a comfortable Sunday afternoon affair.

This guide covers the six best sports bars in Dubai, organised by neighbourhood.

## JBR and Dubai Marina

### Barasti Beach Bar

[Barasti](/venues/barasti-dubai) at Le Meridien Mina Seyahi is the most iconic sports bar in Dubai. Set right on the beach, it sprawls across multiple indoor and outdoor areas with dozens of screens. Friday brunch here during a Premier League matchday is a Dubai rite of passage.

**What makes it special:**

- Beachfront location with outdoor screens
- Massive capacity — it rarely turns people away
- Every major sport covered: EPL, rugby, cricket, F1, UFC
- Friday brunch packages with free-flowing drinks and sport on the screens
- DJ sets after the final whistle on weekends

**Best for:** Premier League, rugby, cricket, UFC, any big event

**Price range:** Upscale ($$$). Pints from 50 AED, brunch packages from 250 AED.

**Rating:** 4.5 / 5

### Lock, Stock & Barrel

[Lock, Stock & Barrel](/venues/lock-stock-barrel-dubai) in JBR is a lively bar-restaurant with a strong sports offering. The venue has multiple large screens and a rowdy, fun atmosphere that picks up quickly on match nights.

**What makes it special:**

- Central JBR location, walkable from the beach
- Regular live music between sporting events
- Good cocktail menu alongside the standard beers
- Reservable booths with dedicated screens for private groups

**Best for:** EPL, Champions League, boxing, UFC

**Price range:** Upscale ($$$). Pints from 55 AED.

**Rating:** 4.3 / 5

## Downtown Dubai

### McGettigan's DWTC

[McGettigan's](/venues/mcgettigans-dubai) at the Dubai World Trade Centre is the anchor of Dubai's Irish pub chain and the best all-round sports bar in the Downtown area. It is the go-to for big events — the venue hosts ticketed Super Bowl and Champions League final parties with buffets and drink deals.

**What makes it special:**

- Huge venue with a capacity of 500+
- Official screening partner for several major sporting events
- Irish pub atmosphere with excellent Guinness
- Regular promotions: ladies' night, quiz night, and match-day specials
- Multiple audio zones so different games can be followed simultaneously

**Best for:** EPL, Six Nations, GAA, Champions League, Super Bowl

**Price range:** Mid-range ($$). Pints from 40 AED, match-day deals available.

**Rating:** 4.4 / 5

### Belgian Beer Cafe

[Belgian Beer Cafe](/venues/belgian-beer-cafe-dubai) at the Crowne Plaza on Sheikh Zayed Road is a relaxed, European-style venue with an impressive beer selection and consistent sports coverage. It is a quieter alternative to the mega-bars and suits fans who want to watch without shouting over a crowd.

**What makes it special:**

- Over 50 Belgian beers on the menu
- Relaxed, low-key atmosphere — great for midweek Champions League nights
- Good food menu with mussels, frites, and steaks
- Several large screens but never overwhelmingly loud

**Best for:** Champions League, Bundesliga, La Liga, Belgian Pro League

**Price range:** Mid-range ($$). Belgian beers from 45 AED.

**Rating:** 4.2 / 5

## Business Bay and Beyond

### Nezesaussi Grill

[Nezesaussi Grill](/venues/nezesaussi-grill-dubai) is a tri-national concept (New Zealand, South Africa, Australia) at the Aloft Hotel in South Dubai. It is the best venue in the city for rugby, cricket, and Aussie sports.

**What makes it special:**

- Kiwi, South African, and Australian sport always prioritised
- Big screens with satellite feeds from SuperSport, Sky NZ, and Fox Sports
- Excellent steaks and South African-inspired menu
- Opens early for Super Rugby and NRL on weekends
- State of Origin and Rugby World Cup are treated as major events

**Best for:** NRL, Super Rugby, AFL, cricket, Rugby World Cup

**Price range:** Mid-range ($$). Pints from 42 AED.

**Rating:** 4.5 / 5

### The Irish Village

[The Irish Village](/venues/the-irish-village-dubai) at the Aviation Club in Garhoud is one of Dubai's oldest licensed venues and a beloved institution among long-term expats. The outdoor garden is one of the most pleasant places in Dubai to watch sport on a cooler evening.

**What makes it special:**

- Iconic outdoor garden with mature trees and fairy lights
- One of the few venues with a genuine neighbourhood-pub feel
- Strong GAA and rugby coverage alongside EPL
- Relaxed, family-friendly atmosphere (especially during the day)
- Reasonable prices by Dubai standards

**Best for:** EPL, GAA, Six Nations, cricket

**Price range:** Mid-range ($$). Pints from 38 AED.

**Rating:** 4.4 / 5

## Practical Tips

### Alcohol in Dubai

Alcohol is only served in licensed venues, which typically means hotels, hotel-affiliated restaurants, and designated bar areas. All six venues in this guide are licensed. You no longer need an alcohol licence as a consumer (the law changed in 2023), but public intoxication remains an offence.

### Getting Around

Dubai is spread out. Barasti and JBR venues are best reached by taxi, Uber, or Dubai Metro (Jumeirah Lakes Towers station). Downtown venues are near the Financial Centre or World Trade Centre metro stops. The Irish Village and Nezesaussi Grill require a taxi.

### Dress Code

Most Dubai sports bars enforce a smart-casual dress code. Football shirts are generally fine on match days, but avoid beachwear or flip-flops at hotel-based venues. Barasti is the exception — beachwear is expected.

### Booking

Reservations are strongly recommended for Friday brunch at Barasti, any big event at McGettigan's, and Champions League nights at Lock, Stock & Barrel. Most venues accept bookings via their websites or by phone.

### Ramadan

During Ramadan (dates shift each year), some venues adjust their hours or stop serving alcohol during daylight fasting hours. Sports bars inside hotels are generally less affected. Check directly with the venue if your visit falls during Ramadan.
