# Where to Watch NFL in London (2026 Guide)

London is one of the best cities outside North America to watch American football. The NFL's International Series has brought games to London since 2007, creating a genuine local fanbase — and with it, a strong network of bars that cater to NFL fans throughout the regular season and playoffs.

## NFL UK Broadcast Times

In the UK, NFL games are broadcast primarily on **Sky Sports NFL**. Selected games are on Channel 4 free-to-air, including some playoffs and the Super Bowl.

| US Kick-Off (ET) | UK Time |
|---|---|
| 1:00 PM Sunday (early) | 6:00 PM Sunday |
| 4:25 PM Sunday (late) | 9:25 PM Sunday |
| 8:20 PM Sunday (night) | 1:20 AM Monday |
| 8:15 PM Monday (MNF) | 1:15 AM Tuesday |
| 8:20 PM Thursday (TNF) | 1:20 AM Friday |

The early window (6:00 PM Sunday) is the most accessible. The late and night games are for dedicated fans only — these are proper late nights.

## Best Areas for NFL Bars in London

### Shoreditch & Liverpool Street

The tech and media crowd — lots of Americans and Canadians. Sports bars here have embraced NFL culture, with several putting on special screenings for big games.

### Covent Garden & West End

High tourist concentration means plenty of American sports bars and Irish pubs with Sky Sports. Easy to find somewhere showing the game if you're in central London.

### Canary Wharf

Financial district with a high American expat population. Several bars in the Wharf area cater explicitly to NFL fans.

### Soho

Central and accessible. A few dedicated American sports bars that go all-in on NFL culture — late-night games, wings, the full experience.

## Super Bowl in London

The Super Bowl is the biggest night of the year for NFL bars in London. Kick-off is typically around 11:30 PM UK time — a late-night occasion. The best bars put on Super Bowl parties with reserved tables, set menus, and genuine big-game atmosphere.

**Book ahead.** Super Bowl bookings at London's top NFL bars fill up weeks in advance. If you want a seat, reserve it the moment you make your plans.

## Tips for Watching NFL in London

- **Sky Sports NFL is the key channel.** Make sure the bar has Sky Sports NFL specifically — not just Sky Sports Main Event.
- **Thursday Night Football is very late.** The TNF kick-off is 1:20 AM UK time. Only dedicated NFL bars stay open for it.
- **The early Sunday window is easiest.** Most Sky Sports pubs will show the 6:00 PM game without any drama.
- **Playoff games draw genuine crowds.** Conference championships and the Super Bowl are real events in London — not niche expat gatherings.
- **American bars vs British pubs.** British pubs with Sky will show the game. American-themed bars will make an event of it. Choose based on the atmosphere you want.
