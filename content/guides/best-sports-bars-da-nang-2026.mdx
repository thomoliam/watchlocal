Da Nang doesn't get talked about enough in the context of watching sport abroad, and that's mostly because the city itself doesn't get talked about enough. It sits in the shadow of Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City in terms of expat reputation, but for quality of life — clean streets, beach, decent infrastructure, low cost of living — it punches well above its weight. The sports bar scene is smaller than Saigon, but it's growing, and the crowd that has landed here over the last few years is football-first.

The key thing to know: Da Nang is UTC+7. That's the same timezone as Bangkok, so Premier League Saturday 3pm = 9pm local. The timing is as good as it gets. You don't need a big scene when the scheduling works this perfectly.

Two main zones. An Thuong is the expat neighbourhood — a loose cluster of streets a few blocks back from My Khe beach where most of the digital nomads and longer-stay foreigners eat, drink, and work. The beach strip along My Khe itself has more bars with screens that cater to a broader tourist crowd. Both are worth knowing. An Thuong is where the regulars are; the beach strip is where you end up when An Thuong is closed or you want something more open-air.

## Golden Pine Pub

The most established sports bar in Da Nang and the reference point for everyone new in town. Golden Pine has multiple screens, cold Bia Hoi (local draught), and a crowd that shows up consistently for Premier League and Champions League. The fit-out is your classic expat pub — nothing flashy, everything functional. The regulars are a mix of long-stay foreigners and visiting football fans who found it on the first night and kept coming back. It gets genuinely busy for big UCL knockouts.

- The most reliable sports bar in the city — screens, crowd, atmosphere
- Good draught beer selection by Da Nang standards
- Champions League nights pack the place out
- Kitchen runs late on match nights
- **Price:** $$
- **Rating:** 4.6/5

## The Lighthouse Bar (An Thuong)

An Thuong's most consistent after-dark option for sport. The Lighthouse is smaller than Golden Pine but has a tighter, more regular crowd — if you're spending more than a week in Da Nang, you'll recognise faces here quickly. It shows EPL without fuss, the staff know when kickoff is before you ask, and the outdoor seating at the front is good for watching on warmer evenings (which is most evenings). Solid option for mid-week matches when Golden Pine is a bit quiet.

- Loyal regular crowd — good for meeting other expats
- Outdoor seating that works well for evening fixtures
- Consistent EPL and UCL coverage
- **Price:** $$
- **Rating:** 4.4/5

## My Khe Beach Bars (strip)

The beach strip along My Khe isn't one venue but a row of open-air bars facing the sea that have screens inside. The best of them — look for the ones with LED screens visible from outside — do a perfectly decent job for weekend EPL. The trade-off is that the atmosphere can be thin outside of high season: you might be watching a top-of-the-table clash with three other people. But on a warm Saturday night with a cold beer and the South China Sea thirty metres away, nobody's complaining.

- Open-air beachside setting — the most pleasant setting on this list
- Good for weekend EPL when tourist numbers are up
- Can be thin on crowd during low season
- **Price:** $$
- **Rating:** 4.2/5

## Sky 36 Bar (Novotel)

The rooftop bar at the Novotel on the beach occasionally sets up screens for big Champions League matches and Premier League events. This is not your regular-season, mid-table-clash venue — but for UCL finals and title deciders, they've run proper events with decent crowd energy and the view from up there makes it memorable. Check their Instagram or Facebook ahead of a big fixture to see if they're running an event. Worth it when they do.

- Rooftop setting with views of Da Nang at night
- Best for major UCL and EPL events — not everyday matches
- More expensive than An Thuong bars but justified for special fixtures
- **Price:** $$$$
- **Rating:** 4.3/5

## New Zealand Bar (An Thuong)

A New Zealand-run bar in the An Thuong area that's become a dependable fixture for the smaller expat community. Rugby gets shown here — proper rugby, not just when a World Cup rolls around — which makes it one of the few places in Vietnam you can reliably watch Six Nations or The Rugby Championship with an engaged crowd. Also does EPL. The owner knows sport and it shows in how the screens are set up and what gets prioritised.

- One of the only places in Da Nang for reliable rugby union coverage
- Owner-operated with genuine sport knowledge
- Good for Six Nations, Rugby Championship, and EPL
- **Price:** $$
- **Rating:** 4.4/5

## Luna Pub (An Thuong)

A newer addition to An Thuong that's building a regular sports-watching following. Luna has good screens, late hours, and is attracting the younger nomad crowd who want to watch the match but also want a bar that feels current rather than like it opened in 2009 and hasn't changed since. Food is better than you'd expect. Gets lively for Saturday EPL nights and has been starting to show Champions League properly.

- More modern fit-out than the older expat staples
- Younger crowd — good for EPL night energy
- Late hours mean it works for mid-week UCL
- **Price:** $$
- **Rating:** 4.3/5

## Bao Bao Bar (near Han River)

A short walk from An Thuong towards the Han River bridge, Bao Bao is the option for when you want to mix watching football with the wider Da Nang riverside scene. Screens inside, busy on weekend nights, cold beer at Vietnamese prices. More tourist-facing than the An Thuong spots but not in a way that kills the atmosphere. Useful to know for when you're on that side of the city and don't want to Grab back to An Thuong for kickoff.

- Good location near the riverside for pre-match drinks
- Works well as a casual EPL watch with a mixed crowd
- Vietnamese beer prices — the cheapest option on this list
- **Price:** $
- **Rating:** 4.1/5

## Practical Tips

**An Thuong is your base.** If you're staying in Da Nang for more than a few days and care about sport, get yourself into the An Thuong area for at least some of your time. The expat community is small enough that you'll know the regulars within a week, and that makes every match better.

**The timing is perfect.** UTC+7 means Saturday 3pm EPL = 9pm local. The city is set up for it without even trying. Early kickoffs (12:30pm UK) land at 6:30pm — happy hour territory. You will not have scheduling problems here.

**The scene is growing.** Da Nang's expat and nomad population has increased significantly in the last few years. New bars with screens keep opening in An Thuong. If a bar you'd heard about has closed or moved, ask at Golden Pine — locals will know the current state of play.

**For big UCL nights, book or go early.** Golden Pine and The Lighthouse aren't massive venues. UCL quarter-finals and semis genuinely fill up. Show up 30 minutes before kickoff if you want a table with a good view.

**Vietnam SIM cards are excellent.** If you're a nomad and want to track pre-match team news or live scores on your phone while watching, get a Viettel or Vinaphone SIM at the airport. Fast, cheap, works everywhere in the city. No need to rely on bar wifi.
