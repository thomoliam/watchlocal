The Premier League is the world's most-watched football league, broadcast in 188 countries. If you've moved abroad or travel frequently, keeping up with your club is a matter of finding the right venue — and knowing when to be there.

This guide covers everything an expat or long-term traveller needs to know about watching the Premier League live, anywhere in the world.

## Premier League Kickoff Times Around the World

The Premier League season runs from mid-August to mid-May. Most games are played on Saturdays and Sundays, with some midweek fixtures. UK kickoff times and their equivalents:

| UK Time | Bangkok | Singapore | Sydney (AEDT) | New York | Dubai |
|---------|---------|-----------|---------------|----------|-------|
| 12:30 GMT | 19:30 | 20:30 | 23:30 | 07:30 | 16:30 |
| 15:00 GMT | 22:00 | 23:00 | 02:00+1 | 10:00 | 19:00 |
| 17:30 GMT | 00:30+1 | 01:30+1 | 04:30+1 | 12:30 | 21:30 |
| 20:00 GMT (midweek) | 03:00+1 | 04:00+1 | 07:00+1 | 15:00 | 00:00+1 |

*+1 indicates the following day. AEDT applies during Australian summer (Oct–Apr); AEST (GMT+10) applies winter.*

Note: The 15:00 Saturday fixtures are subject to the **UK broadcasting blackout** — they cannot be shown legally on UK TV, but international broadcast rights are not restricted. Sports bars abroad with the correct international subscriptions can and do show these matches.

## How to Find Premier League Bars Abroad

The challenge with Premier League abroad isn't the number of venues — there are thousands globally — it's finding one with:

1. **The correct broadcast subscription** for your location. Broadcasting rights are sold country-by-country. A bar in Bangkok needs a Thai broadcaster with EPL rights; a bar in Singapore needs a different package entirely.

2. **Good screen setup.** Not all sports bars are equal. A Premier League game deserves a big screen, good sound, and enough screens to cover different viewing angles.

3. **The right atmosphere.** If you're a passionate fan, you want to be around other passionate fans — not a venue where the game is background noise.

## The Premier League Abroad in Southeast Asia

Southeast Asia has the most passionate Premier League following outside the UK. In Bangkok, Singapore, Kuala Lumpur, and Ho Chi Minh City, EPL coverage starts in the evening (for the early Saturday kickoff) and runs into the early hours for the late games.

**Bangkok** is the standout city — Sukhumvit and Silom both have dedicated sports bars that open through the night for Premier League fixtures. Some venues have cultivated specific club followings, which creates a genuine fans-bar atmosphere.

**Singapore** has world-class sports bars in Clarke Quay and Holland Village with premium broadcast setups and extensive screen coverage.

**Bali** is worth mentioning for travellers: despite being a tourist destination, Seminyak and Kuta have several sports bars that show every EPL game, catering to Australian and British visitors.

## The Premier League in the Middle East

Dubai has a large British expat community and strong Premier League coverage. The Dubai Sports City area and JBR (Jumeirah Beach Residence) both have multiple sports bars showing EPL. Kickoff times work well for Dubai — the 12:30 UK game is a civilised 4:30pm, and the 17:30 game is a 9:30pm evening watch.

Abu Dhabi and Doha have similar infrastructure, particularly since the region's connection to Premier League ownership increased awareness and fan engagement.

## The Premier League in Australia

Australian fans have arguably the most complicated relationship with Premier League timing. The early Saturday kickoff (12:30 UK) is a midnight watch for Sydney-based fans (23:30 AEDT). The 15:00 game runs to 2am. The 17:30 game is a 4:30am Sunday morning alarm.

Despite this, Australian EPL fans are famously dedicated. Sydney and Melbourne both have sports bars that open at midnight specifically for the early Saturday game, with a core group of regulars who treat it as a pre-dawn ritual. Bondi and Coogee in Sydney, and Fitzroy and Collingwood in Melbourne, are the places to look.

## What to Ask When Finding a Venue

Before committing to a sports bar for Premier League, it's worth checking:

- **"Do you show all Premier League games, or just selected fixtures?"** — Some bars only show the biggest matches. If you follow a mid-table club, verify your game will be on.
- **"What's the sound situation?"** — Some pubs show games muted during quieter periods. For the Premier League, you want commentary.
- **"Can I request the screen nearest my table?"** — Useful when multiple games are showing simultaneously.
- **"Do you take reservations?"** — Essential for Manchester United, Liverpool, Arsenal, or Chelsea away games, and any Derby.

## Premier League Community Abroad

One of the underrated aspects of following the Premier League abroad is the community you find. Most major cities have official supporter clubs for the big clubs — Arsenal America, Chelsea Asia, Liverpool groups across Southeast Asia. These groups often have designated venues, which means a passionate crowd for every match.
