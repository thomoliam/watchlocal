Bangkok is one of the best cities in the world for watching live sport. The timezone lines up perfectly with European football, Aussie rules kicks off at sociable evening hours, and UFC events land on Sunday mornings — ideal for a boozy brunch. Whether you are an expat who has made Sukhumvit home or a traveller passing through, there is a sports bar for every fan.

This guide covers the eight best sports bars in Bangkok, organised by what they do best.

## Best Overall — The Sportsman Bar & Restaurant

[The Sportsman](/venues/the-sportsman-bangkok) is the undisputed king of sports bars in Bangkok and one of the best in Southeast Asia. Spread across two floors on Sukhumvit Soi 13, the venue packs in 8 HD projectors and more than 26 LED screens. If a sport is being broadcast anywhere on the planet, The Sportsman is probably showing it.

**What makes it special:**

- Two floors with separate audio zones so you can follow two events at once
- Opens early for 6 a.m. Premier League kickoffs (Saturday and Sunday mornings in Bangkok)
- Full English breakfasts served from opening
- Dedicated pool tables and darts area downstairs
- Strong Wi-Fi and charging points at every booth

**Best for:** Premier League, Champions League, Six Nations, F1, UFC, NFL Super Bowl, and any major event you can name.

**Price range:** Mid-range ($$). Pints from 150 baht, meals from 250 baht.

**Rating:** 4.7 / 5

## Best for EPL — The Robin Hood

[The Robin Hood](/venues/the-robin-hood-bangkok) on Sukhumvit Soi 33 is a proper British pub that has been pulling pints since the late 1990s. It is the go-to for die-hard Premier League fans and the venue of choice for several Bangkok-based supporters groups.

**What makes it special:**

- Official home to multiple EPL fan clubs in Bangkok
- Full English breakfast served from 7 a.m. on match mornings
- Traditional pub quiz every Wednesday
- Reliable streams with minimal buffering — they use satellite feeds, not IPTV
- Sunday roast with all the trimmings

**Best for:** Premier League, FA Cup, Champions League, Six Nations rugby

**Price range:** Mid-range ($$). Pints from 140 baht.

**Rating:** 4.4 / 5

## Best for UFC and MMA — Hooters Bangkok

[Hooters Bangkok](/venues/hooters-bangkok) on Sukhumvit Soi 15 has become the unofficial home of UFC viewing in Bangkok. Every numbered UFC event is screened live, typically kicking off at 10 a.m. Sunday morning Bangkok time — perfect timing for wings and beer.

**What makes it special:**

- Always shows UFC main cards on the big screens
- UFC brunch specials with discounted wings and pitchers
- Multiple large-format screens visible from every seat
- Late-night fight companions welcome (they stay open late for US-timed boxing cards)

**Best for:** UFC, boxing, WWE, NFL

**Price range:** Mid-range ($$). Wings from 199 baht, pitchers from 399 baht.

**Rating:** 4.3 / 5

## Best for Groups — Stumble Inn

[Stumble Inn](/venues/stumble-inn-bangkok) on Sukhumvit Soi Nana is a two-storey venue with an enormous ground-floor bar area that can comfortably seat large groups. It is a favourite among stag parties, corporate outings, and travelling fans looking for a lively atmosphere.

**What makes it special:**

- Huge open-plan ground floor with space for 200+ people
- 20+ screens across two levels
- Dedicated section for darts and pool
- Happy hour from 2 p.m. to 7 p.m. daily
- Live music on non-match nights

**Best for:** EPL, NRL, NFL, any event where you want a crowd atmosphere

**Price range:** Budget ($). Draught beer from 99 baht during happy hour.

**Rating:** 4.2 / 5

## Best Budget-Friendly — The Kiwi Sports Pub

[The Kiwi Sports Pub](/venues/the-kiwi-bangkok) on Sukhumvit Soi 8 is run by New Zealanders and punches well above its weight for the price. The venue is smaller and more intimate than the mega-bars, making it perfect for a quiet session watching the rugby.

**What makes it special:**

- Famous 395-baht Sunday carvery
- Strong coverage of NRL, Super Rugby, and All Blacks tests
- Pool table and a loyal crew of regulars
- One of the cheapest pint prices in the Sukhumvit area
- Kiwi and Aussie snacks behind the bar

**Best for:** NRL, Super Rugby, All Blacks, AFL, cricket

**Price range:** Budget ($). Pints from 99 baht.

**Rating:** 4.5 / 5

## Best for Aussie Sports — Bully's Pub

[Bully's Pub](/venues/bullys-pub-bangkok) on Sukhumvit Soi 4 is a long-standing favourite among Australian expats. It opens early on weekends for AFL and NRL and serves a full Aussie-style breakfast.

**What makes it special:**

- Opens at 5 a.m. for Friday-night NRL and AFL matches (which kick off Saturday morning in Bangkok)
- Meat pies, sausage rolls, and other Aussie comfort food
- Large projector screen plus several smaller TVs
- Friendly, welcoming atmosphere — regulars will happily adopt newcomers for the morning session
- State of Origin and AFL Grand Final are treated as major events

**Best for:** AFL, NRL, cricket, A-League

**Price range:** Budget ($). Beers from 89 baht.

**Rating:** 4.3 / 5

## Best for American Sports — The Sportsman (again) and Hooters

There is no dedicated American sports bar in Bangkok, but both [The Sportsman](/venues/the-sportsman-bangkok) and [Hooters](/venues/hooters-bangkok) reliably screen NFL, NBA, and MLB. For the Super Bowl, The Sportsman hosts a ticketed event with a buffet and drink packages.

**Key tip:** NFL games kick off at 1 a.m. Monday morning (Bangkok time) for the Sunday afternoon US slate, and 5 a.m. Monday for Sunday Night Football. The Sportsman opens for Monday Night Football at noon on Tuesday (Bangkok time). Plan your sleep accordingly.

## Best Rooftop Option — Offshore Bar

[Offshore Bar](/venues/offshore-bar-bangkok) on Sukhumvit Soi 11 offers a rooftop terrace with screens — a rarity in Bangkok's sports bar scene. It is a solid pick on a cooler evening when you want fresh air and football.

**What makes it special:**

- Rooftop terrace with outdoor screens
- Cocktail menu alongside the standard beer selection
- Popular with a younger, mixed crowd
- Live DJ sets after the final whistle on weekends

**Best for:** EPL, La Liga, Champions League — atmosphere over volume

**Price range:** Mid-range ($$). Cocktails from 250 baht.

**Rating:** 4.1 / 5

## Practical Tips for Watching Sport in Bangkok

### Getting There

Most of the top sports bars are clustered along Sukhumvit Road between BTS Nana and BTS Asok stations. A short walk or a cheap Grab ride from either station will get you to any venue in this guide.

### Kickoff Times in Bangkok (ICT, UTC+7)

| League | Typical Kickoff (Bangkok) |
|--------|--------------------------|
| Premier League | 6 p.m. – 12:30 a.m. (Sat/Sun) |
| Champions League | 2 a.m. (Wed/Thu) |
| NFL (Sunday slate) | 1 a.m. – 7 a.m. (Mon) |
| AFL | 5:30 p.m. – 10:30 p.m. (Fri/Sat/Sun) |
| NRL | 5 p.m. – 10 p.m. (Fri/Sat/Sun) |
| UFC (Main Card) | 10 a.m. – 2 p.m. (Sun) |
| Formula 1 | Varies by race location |

### Happy Hours

Most Bangkok sports bars run a happy hour from mid-afternoon to early evening. If you are catching a late European kickoff, arrive early and take advantage of 2-for-1 deals before the whistle blows.

### Booking

For big events (Champions League final, Super Bowl, State of Origin), it is worth booking a table in advance. The Sportsman and Robin Hood both take reservations via their Facebook pages.

### What to Expect

Bangkok sports bars are generally welcoming to all fans. Kit-wearing is common and encouraged. Most venues have English-speaking staff and menus in English. Smoking is prohibited indoors by Thai law, but many bars have outdoor areas where smoking is permitted.
