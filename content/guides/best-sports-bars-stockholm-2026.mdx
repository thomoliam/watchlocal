Stockholm is Scandinavia's biggest city, and its expat sports bar scene reflects that scale. The city has all the same CET timezone advantages as Copenhagen — EPL at 1:30pm and 4pm on Saturdays, Champions League at a civilised 9pm on weeknights — combined with a larger city, a more established international community, and the presence of O'Learys, the Scandinavian sports bar chain that has made reliable football coverage a standard feature of the region.

The city's geography shapes where you'll watch. Södermalm is the hip, creative island where The Waxing Moon has built its following. Norrmalm and the city centre are where O'Learys and the larger venues sit. Everywhere is connected by an excellent metro system.

## O'Learys — Multiple Locations

O'Learys is the dominant sports bar brand in Scandinavia and Stockholm is its home territory. The chain has multiple locations across the city, all following the same reliable formula: large screens, comprehensive sports coverage, American-influenced sports bar layout, dependable food menu. It is not attempting to be a neighbourhood local and doesn't pretend otherwise.

What O'Learys offers is scale and reliability. If you don't know Stockholm and need to find EPL coverage quickly, any O'Learys will have it. The midweek Champions League, weekend NFL, NBA regular season — O'Learys covers all of it consistently. When other venues are full for a major fixture, O'Learys usually has space.

## The Waxing Moon — Södermalm

The Waxing Moon is the answer for those who find O'Learys too large and American in feel. It's a British pub on Södermalm — Stockholm's most characterful island neighbourhood — and it's built a loyal following among the British expat community and Swedes who prefer a more traditional pub atmosphere for their football.

Södermalm's combination of independent shops, restaurants, and bars makes it the most enjoyable part of Stockholm to spend a Saturday, and The Waxing Moon is the natural anchor for anyone doing it properly. It gets busy for major EPL fixtures, so arrive with enough time to find a good spot.

## Connolly's Irish Pub

Connolly's is Stockholm's most established Irish pub for football coverage, and it has the match-day credentials to go with that. It attracts a mix of Irish, British, and Swedish regulars, covers EPL and Champions League consistently, and operates as a proper pub rather than a theme bar.

It's worth knowing for midweek Champions League as well as the standard Saturday EPL. The atmosphere on a big European night is notably better here than at the larger sports bar chains.

## The Black & Blue — Norrmalm

The Black & Blue operates in the central Norrmalm district and works well as a sports bar that doesn't over-commit to any single football identity. It's a reliable choice for EPL, American sport, and the broader international calendar. The Norrmalm location makes it easy to combine with the rest of the city centre, and it handles large crowds better than some of the smaller pubs.

Useful to know for the occasions when your preferred venue is packed and you need a solid backup that won't disappoint.

## Bishops Arms

Bishops Arms is a hotel bar chain with locations across Stockholm, and it punches above what you'd typically expect from a hotel venue. The outlets generally have good screens, reliable sport coverage, and the kind of consistent standards that come from being part of a larger operation. For travellers staying in Stockholm hotels rather than navigating the city's pub geography from scratch, Bishops Arms is the sensible default.

The sport coverage is comprehensive — EPL, Champions League, internationals — and the quality varies by location but is reliable across the chain.

## Dublin Arms

Dublin Arms is the smaller, more local Irish pub option on the Stockholm circuit. It's the kind of venue regulars end up at when the better-known places are too busy, and they tend to stay because the atmosphere is right. It draws a consistent football crowd and covers the Premier League reliably, without the crowds you'll sometimes encounter at Connolly's for a top-six fixture.

Worth knowing as a backup, and worth knowing as a local recommendation if you're in Stockholm long enough to want a regular.

## Practical Tips

**CET is perfect for football.** Stockholm has arguably the best EPL timezone of any city outside the UK and Ireland. The Saturday afternoon schedule maps directly onto what you'd experience at home, and the weeknight Champions League timing requires no adjustment. For British expats, the timezone is one of Stockholm's genuine advantages.

**O'Learys as a safety net.** The chain's multiple city locations mean you're never far from reliable sport coverage. It lacks the atmosphere of The Waxing Moon or Connolly's, but for any fixture where you don't want to risk being turned away from a smaller venue, O'Learys is the answer.

**Södermalm is worth the effort.** The Waxing Moon is on Södermalm, and spending a Saturday afternoon there — match and then exploring the neighbourhood — is a genuinely good use of a Stockholm weekend. The island is one of the more pleasant places in Scandinavia to spend an afternoon.

**Swedish football credentials.** Sweden has produced world-class footballers — Ibrahimović being the obvious but far from only example — and the country's relationship with the Premier League is long and informed. Swedes in sports bars generally know their football. The Allsvenskan season runs summer to autumn (European schedule), which means the Swedish league is in full swing during EPL's August to May window, creating a genuinely football-saturated culture.

**NBA has a growing audience.** Swedish NBA players have given the country a connection to basketball that goes beyond casual interest. Gobert's presence in recent years has kept that engaged. O'Learys particularly handles NBA coverage well — the American sports bar format suits it perfectly.

**Booking for big matches.** Stockholm's expat community is large but the best football venues are still relatively small. For a Manchester derby or a major Champions League match, reserving a spot at The Waxing Moon or Connolly's is sensible planning.
