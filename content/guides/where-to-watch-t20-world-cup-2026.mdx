# Where to Watch the ICC T20 World Cup 2026 Abroad

The ICC T20 World Cup 2026 is hosted across India and Sri Lanka from June through July 2026. For the hundreds of millions of cricket fans living outside the subcontinent, that means figuring out when to watch — and where to find a venue that actually shows it.

Good news: the timezone maths work in your favour for most of the world.

## The Tournament Calendar

Group stage matches run through June, with knockout rounds and the final expected in late June or early July 2026. Matches are played in Indian Standard Time (IST), which is UTC+5:30. India and Sri Lanka host multiple venues, with the biggest matches likely in Mumbai, Delhi, Colombo, and other major cricketing cities.

Expect match times around 3pm IST and 7pm IST — two standard slots for ICC tournaments.

## The Timezone Breakdown

IST (UTC+5:30) lands in a sweet spot for most of the world outside the Americas:

**Afternoon match (3pm IST):**
- London (UTC): 9:30am
- Dubai (GST): 1:30pm
- Bangkok (ICT): 2:30pm
- Singapore / KL: 4:30pm
- Sydney / Melbourne (AEST): 6:30–7pm
- New York (EDT): 5:30am — hard watch

**Evening match (7pm IST):**
- London: 1:30pm
- Dubai: 3:30pm
- Bangkok: 5:30pm
- Singapore / KL: 6:30pm
- Sydney / Melbourne: 9:30–10pm
- New York: 9:30am

Europe, the Middle East, and Southeast Asia all land in genuinely civilised viewing windows. Australia gets evening cricket. The US is the outlier — early morning starts for most East Coast viewers.

## Who's Watching

The T20 World Cup draws the largest cricket audience on Earth, but the viewership is concentrated within specific diaspora communities:

- **South Asian diaspora** (Indian, Pakistani, Bangladeshi, Sri Lankan) across the UK, UAE, Singapore, Malaysia, and Australia
- **English and Australian cricket fans** — both nations are serious T20 contenders
- **Caribbean fans** — West Indies have a strong T20 tradition

In cities with large South Asian communities, the T20 World Cup isn't a niche event — it's appointment viewing that rivals the football World Cup in intensity.

## Best Cities to Watch

**Dubai** is arguably the single best city outside the subcontinent for watching ICC cricket. The UAE has one of the highest proportions of South Asian residents of any country in the world, a genuine cricket culture, and afternoon match times (1:30–3:30pm GST) that are perfectly placed for venue viewing. Shisha lounges, sports bars, and community clubs around Bur Dubai, Deira, and the newer areas of Dubai all show ICC matches.

## The Match You Cannot Miss: India vs Pakistan

Every ICC tournament features one unmissable fixture: India vs Pakistan. It is the most-watched cricket match on Earth, and the atmosphere in diaspora cities for this game is unlike anything else in sport.

In Dubai, shisha lounges and Indian restaurants will be standing room only. In London, community venues and cricket clubs book out days in advance. In Singapore, anywhere with a screen and a South Asian clientele will be packed.

If you want to watch India vs Pakistan at a venue, **book in advance**. Don't rely on walking in.

## How to Find a Venue Showing Cricket

This is the real challenge. Most mainstream sports bars don't advertise cricket — you need to know where to look:

1. **Search WatchLocal for cricket** in your city to find venues with confirmed coverage
2. **Call ahead and ask specifically** — ask whether the venue has Willow TV, ICC's broadcast partner in your region, or a dedicated cricket channel
3. **Look at South Asian restaurants and Indian/Pakistani community venues** — they often show matches even without a formal sports bar setup
4. **Check cricket club websites** — many cricket clubs in the UK, Australia, and Singapore host public viewing events for ICC tournaments

The T20 World Cup is a month-long tournament. Once you find a venue that shows it for the group stage, you've found your base for the knockouts and final.
