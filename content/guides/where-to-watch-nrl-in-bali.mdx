# Where to Watch NRL in Bali (2026 Guide)

Whether you're a Broncos tragic who can't miss a Friday night game or a Storm fan keeping tabs on the ladder from a sun lounger, Bali has you covered. The island has a well-established Aussie expat and tourist scene, and with it comes a solid network of sports bars that show NRL live — even at the early morning kick-off times that come with watching Australian sport from Indonesia.

This guide covers the best venues across Seminyak, Canggu, Kuta, Legian, and Ubud, plus what you need to know about timing, streaming rights, and which bars are most reliable for getting every game.

## What Time Does NRL Kick Off in Bali?

Bali runs on Central Indonesia Time (WITA), which is UTC+8 — one hour behind Queensland and two hours behind NSW/Victoria during non-daylight saving months.

| NRL Kick-Off (AEST) | Bali Time (WITA) |
|---|---|
| 6:00 PM Thursday | 4:00 PM |
| 7:50 PM Thursday | 5:50 PM |
| 3:00 PM Saturday | 1:00 PM |
| 5:30 PM Saturday | 3:30 PM |
| 7:35 PM Saturday | 5:35 PM |
| 4:05 PM Sunday | 2:05 PM |
| 6:20 PM Sunday | 4:20 PM |

The good news: NRL times are actually perfect for watching in Bali. Most games fall between early afternoon and early evening local time — so you can watch the footy, then head out for dinner. No late nights, no 3am alarm calls.

## Best Areas to Watch NRL in Bali

### Seminyak

The highest concentration of sports bars in Bali is in Seminyak. Venues on and around Jalan Kayu Aya, Jalan Petitenget, and Seminyak Square reliably show NRL throughout the season.

### Canggu

Canggu has become Bali's digital nomad hub and the sports bar scene has grown with it. More laid-back than Seminyak, with a younger crowd. Good for watching during the day.

### Kuta & Legian

The original tourist strip. Plenty of options around Jalan Pantai Kuta and Poppies Lane, especially for Grand Final season when bars put big signs out front advertising the coverage.

### Ubud

Options are more limited but they exist. Good for travellers staying inland who don't want to trek to the coast just for a game.

## Tips for Watching NRL in Bali

- **Book ahead for big games.** The NRL Grand Final and State of Origin games fill up fast. Arrive early and ask about reservations for major fixtures.
- **Arrive 15-20 minutes early.** Good seats in front of the main screens go fast, especially on Saturday afternoons.
- **Check the schedule.** Most bars show the NRL schedule on a chalkboard outside. Walk the strip the day before a big game to see who's advertising coverage.
- **Sound is everything.** Some bars show sport on mute. If audio matters to you, ask before you sit down.
- **Bring cash.** Many smaller Bali sports bars are cash-only or add a surcharge for card payments.

## NRL Grand Final in Bali

If you're lucky enough to be in Bali for the NRL Grand Final (typically first Sunday in October), you're in for a great day. The Aussie community in Bali turns the Grand Final into a genuine event — bars fill up, jerseys come out, and the atmosphere is genuinely electric. Book your spot at least a day in advance.

## State of Origin in Bali

State of Origin is arguably the biggest event in the Bali sports bar calendar. Three games across May–July draw massive crowds of Queensland and NSW fans. If you're in Bali during Origin, book your table at least a day in advance — these games are serious business.
