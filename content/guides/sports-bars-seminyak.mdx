# Sports Bars in Seminyak, Bali — Complete Guide (2026)

Seminyak is where Bali's sports bar scene is most concentrated. With a high density of Australian tourists and a strong expat community, the streets around Jalan Kayu Aya and Jalan Petitenget are lined with venues showing live sport from around the world — NRL, AFL, Premier League, UFC, and more.

## What Sport Can You Watch in Seminyak?

Seminyak sports bars typically show:
- **NRL** — all games, including Thursday nights, the weekend slate, State of Origin, and the Grand Final
- **AFL** — Saturday and Sunday games throughout the premiership season and finals
- **Premier League** — all weekend fixtures and midweek Champions League games
- **UFC** — major Fight Nights and PPV events
- **F1, cricket, tennis** — major events depending on the venue

## Key Streets for Sports Bars in Seminyak

### Jalan Kayu Aya (Eat Street)

The main drag. Multiple bars along here show sport and most are open from lunch through late night. Easy to walk between venues if one is full.

### Jalan Petitenget

Slightly more upmarket. A good mix of sports bars and restaurants, with several venues regularly showing NRL and AFL.

### Seminyak Square (Jalan Laksmana/Oberoi)

Home to some of Bali's most dedicated sports venues. Fully air-conditioned with multiple screens facing every direction.

### Jalan Camplung Tanduk

A quieter street with a few reliable options popular with NRL and AFL crowds.

## When Is Sport On in Seminyak?

Because Indonesia sits in WITA (UTC+8), Australian sport times translate well:
- **NRL:** Thursday evenings, Saturday and Sunday afternoons
- **AFL:** Saturday and Sunday mornings through afternoon
- **Premier League:** Saturday and Sunday evenings (UK is 7 hours behind Bali)
- **UFC:** Late night/early morning — US time zones mean main events often start around midnight Bali time

## Tips for Watching Sport in Seminyak

**Check the chalkboard.** Before any big game, walk the strip the previous evening. Bars advertise their upcoming coverage on boards outside — it's the fastest way to see who has the rights.

**Book for big games.** NRL Grand Final, AFL Grand Final, and State of Origin games fill up quickly. Contact the bar a day or two ahead.

**Ask about sound.** Some bars show sport on mute. If commentary matters to you, check before you sit down.

**Arrive early for prime spots.** The best seats in front of the main screen go fast. Aim to be there 20-30 minutes before kick-off.

**Not all bars have rights.** A small number of venues have paid for genuine sport streaming packages. If a bar seems vague about whether they can show a specific competition, they probably can't.
