# Where to Watch the NBA Finals 2026 Abroad

The NBA Finals tips off in June 2026. For fans watching from outside the United States, that means one thing: a timezone puzzle. Depending on where you are in the world, Game 1 could be a Saturday morning brunch or a punishing 3am alarm. Here's how to figure out your viewing situation — and where to find a bar that's actually showing it.

## The Timezone Problem

NBA Finals games typically start around 8:30–9pm Eastern Time. That single number translates to wildly different local times depending on where you're sitting:

- **London (BST):** 2am
- **Paris / Berlin (CEST):** 3am
- **Dubai (GST):** 5am
- **Bangkok (ICT):** 8am
- **Singapore / Kuala Lumpur (SGT/MYT):** 9am
- **Sydney / Melbourne (AEST):** 11am
- **Tokyo / Seoul:** 10am

Europe draws the short straw. Asia and Australia are the clear winners.

## Who Benefits: SE Asia and Australia

If you're in Bangkok, Singapore, Sydney, or Melbourne, the NBA Finals are genuinely great morning sport. A 9pm ET tip-off lands in the 8–11am window locally — which lines up with weekend brunch, a coffee, or a lazy morning start. This is a legitimate viewing event, not a sacrifice.

**Bangkok** has a strong sports bar culture on Sukhumvit, and American sports — NFL, NBA, MLB — are reliably covered at the bigger venues. The NBA Finals in June falls on a weekend morning, making it one of the most sociable watch experiences in the city.

**Singapore** is similar. Clarke Quay and the CBD are home to sports bars that hold the right broadcast licenses and stay open for NBA content. Morning NBA viewing during the playoffs is an established ritual for the city's American expat community.

**Sydney and Melbourne** benefit from the AEST timezone all season. By Finals time in June, a Saturday morning game feels almost made for a sports bar brunch session.

## Europe: Late Nights, Dedicated Bars

London, Amsterdam, Berlin, and Paris all face a 2–3am start. It's brutal. But every major European city has a handful of bars that cater specifically to this — venues that stay open late specifically for American sports, equipped with ESPN or NBA League Pass commercial feeds.

In **London**, look for venues in Shoreditch, Soho, and around the major expat corridors. These bars know their audience and will advertise Finals coverage in advance. Check WatchLocal for NBA listings in London the week before each game to see which venues have confirmed they're opening late.

## Tokyo and Seoul: Morning Local Time

**Tokyo** (10am JST) and **Seoul** (10am KST) sit in an interesting middle ground — early enough to feel like morning viewing, late enough to be a real event. Japan and South Korea both have strong NBA fanbases, and dedicated sports bars in Shibuya or Itaewon will show Finals games.
