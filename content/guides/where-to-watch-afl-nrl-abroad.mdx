Ask any Australian expat what they miss most about home, and sport is almost always on the list. AFL and NRL are deeply ingrained in Australian culture — following them from abroad, however, requires finding venues with the right broadcast subscriptions, which can be surprisingly difficult.

This guide covers where to find Australian sport — AFL and NRL primarily — in major expat destinations around the world.

## Why AFL and NRL Are Hard to Find Abroad

Unlike the Premier League or Champions League, which have lucrative broadcast deals in virtually every country, AFL and NRL international rights are more limited. Some countries have no legitimate broadcast outlet at all, meaning sports bars rely on streaming services or Australian satellite feeds rather than local TV packages.

This makes finding a reliable venue more important than usual — and once you find one, it's worth becoming a regular.

## The Best Cities for Watching Australian Sport Abroad

### Bali

Bali is the most obvious destination for Australian sport abroad. With hundreds of thousands of Australians visiting or living in Bali at any given time, the demand for AFL and NRL is consistently met. Seminyak and Canggu both have dedicated sports bars that show every AFL and NRL game throughout the season.

The timing is ideal: Australian sport kicks off in the Bali evening (6pm–10pm local time for most weekend fixtures), which makes it the most comfortable place in Asia to watch.

### Bangkok

Bangkok has a significant Australian expat and tourist population, and the city's sports bar scene has grown to accommodate them. Venues in Sukhumvit and Silom show AFL and NRL alongside their European football coverage. The timing is slightly later — games typically run from 8pm–midnight local time — but Bangkok's late-night culture makes this very manageable.

### Singapore

Singapore has one of the most organised Australian expat communities in Southeast Asia, and the city's sports bars cater accordingly. Clarke Quay and Holland Village both have venues that show AFL and NRL consistently. Some bars host dedicated AFL viewing events during the finals series.

The Australian sporting calendar (AFL finals in September, NRL Grand Final in early October) lines up well with Singapore's evening — games typically kick off from 9pm–11pm local time.

### London

London has a large and active Australian expat community, particularly in areas like Clapham, Shepherd's Bush, and Earls Court. Several Aussie bars in these neighbourhoods operate specifically to serve Australian and New Zealand sports fans — showing AFL, NRL, rugby union, and cricket.

The timing for AFL and NRL in London can be brutal: a 7:30pm AEST game kicks off at 9:30am UK time on a Saturday morning. Despite this, London's Australian sports bars draw a dedicated early-morning crowd with coffee and bacon rolls.

### Dubai and Abu Dhabi

The Gulf states have a significant Australian expat population, particularly in construction and healthcare. Dubai sports bars show AFL and NRL — Jumeirah Beach Residence (JBR) and Dubai Sports City are the best areas to look. Games kick off in the Dubai evening, which is one of the better time zones for watching Australian sport live.

### New York and Los Angeles

The US has a smaller but growing Australian community, and finding AFL/NRL in the US is more of a challenge than in Asia or the Middle East. In New York and LA, look for dedicated Australian bars — they exist, but you'll need to search specifically. An American sports bar is unlikely to have the right subscription unless it's explicitly Australian-focused.

## AFL Fixtures and Season Calendar

The AFL Home and Away season runs from **March to August**, with finals (the "September Finals" — though the schedule often runs into early October) taking place through September to October.

Key dates:
- **Round 1**: Third week of March
- **Finals Series**: Starts early September
- **AFL Grand Final**: Last Saturday of September (usually)

Games in Australia typically kick off at 1:45pm, 4:35pm, and 7:25pm AEST/AEDT on Saturdays and Sundays.

## NRL Fixtures and Season Calendar

The NRL Home and Away season runs from **March to late September**, with finals held in September and the Grand Final in early October.

Key dates:
- **Round 1**: Second or third week of March
- **Finals Series**: Second week of September
- **NRL Grand Final**: First Sunday of October

## Tips for Watching AFL and NRL Abroad

- **Confirm AFL/NRL is actually shown.** Many sports bars advertise "Australian sport" but only reliably show cricket. Ask specifically about AFL and NRL before planning around a venue.
- **Ask about streaming quality.** Some overseas venues use streaming services rather than satellite feeds, which can mean lower quality or buffering during peak times.
- **Build a relationship with the venue.** Once you find a bar that shows your competition reliably, become a regular. They'll hold a good spot for you and make sure the game is on.
- **Check both AFL and NRL options.** In some cities, one sport is much better served than the other. Don't assume that because a venue shows AFL it also shows NRL, or vice versa.
- **Follow expat community groups.** Australian Facebook and WhatsApp groups in most major cities will tell you exactly where to watch — often with reviews of specific venues.
