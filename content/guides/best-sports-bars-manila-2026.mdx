# Best Sports Bars in Manila for Live Sport (2026)

Manila is one of the most sports-obsessed cities in Asia — and it's a basketball city first, last, and always. The PBA (Philippine Basketball Association) is more than a league; it's a cultural institution. NBA games draw near-religious devotion, with fans who'll set alarms for early-morning tip-offs without a second thought. Alongside basketball, there's a massive and growing appetite for Premier League football, UFC, and — among the large American expat and OFW (Overseas Filipino Worker) returnee community — NFL.

The geography matters. **BGC (Bonifacio Global City)** is where you'll find the highest concentration of quality sports bars, catering to Manila's expat professionals and upwardly mobile locals. **Makati** is the established business and expat district with a cluster of Irish pubs and international bars. **Quezon City** and areas like Katipunan offer a more local, neighbourhood feel.

**Time zone note:** Manila runs UTC+8. NBA Sunday games (9pm ET Saturday) tip off at 9am Sunday morning — a perfectly reasonable brunch viewing window. EPL's Saturday 3pm fixture lands at 11pm local, which suits Manila's late-night culture well. For UFC main events (10pm ET), expect a very early Sunday morning in Manila.

## The Best Sports Bars in Manila

### Draft Sports Bar & Grill — BGC

**The pick for NBA and basketball.** Draft is purpose-built for the serious basketball fan: high-definition screens covering every angle, a menu that works for extended viewing sessions, and a crowd that actually knows what it's watching. On NBA play-off nights and Finals games, it fills out with fans who've been tracking the season — not just people who wandered in. PBA games get full attention here too, which gives it a local authenticity some expat-facing bars lack.

- **Best for:** NBA, PBA, Premier League
- **Location:** BGC, Taguig
- **Vibe:** Sports-focused, professional crowd, energetic on big nights
- **Price:** ₱₱ (mid-range)
- **Rating:** 4.5/5

### Handlebar — BGC

A BGC fixture with a loyal regular crowd and the screen setup to justify the loyalty. Handlebar shows up for Premier League, Champions League, and American sport with consistency — which in Manila means a lot, given how patchy coverage can be at smaller venues. The bar itself is well-run, the food is solid, and it's the kind of place where you can arrive alone and find yourself drawn into conversation by halftime. Good for rugby and NFL as well as football.

- **Best for:** Premier League, Champions League, NFL, rugby
- **Location:** BGC, Taguig
- **Vibe:** Casual expat bar, consistent regulars
- **Price:** ₱₱ (mid-range)
- **Rating:** 4.3/5

### Draft Gastropub — Makati

The Makati sibling of the BGC Draft, this one sits closer to the Poblacion neighbourhood and draws a slightly different crowd — more mixed local and expat, slightly less corporate. The screens and programming are at the same level, with basketball coverage at the core and international sport filling the rest. For anyone staying or working in Makati, this is the most convenient option for big match nights.

- **Best for:** NBA, PBA, EPL
- **Location:** Makati CBD
- **Vibe:** Gastropub atmosphere, mixed local/expat
- **Price:** ₱₱ (mid-range)
- **Rating:** 4.2/5

### Route 196 — Katipunan, Quezon City

A genuine local institution rather than an expat-facing sports bar. Route 196 is primarily a live music venue, but sport — especially basketball — is woven into its identity in a way that reflects Quezon City's neighbourhood character. Screens aren't as numerous as the BGC options, but the crowd energy on a big PBA or NBA night is something different: authentically Filipino rather than internationally curated. Worth visiting for that reason alone.

- **Best for:** PBA, NBA, local fixtures
- **Location:** Katipunan Ave, Quezon City
- **Vibe:** Local bar, music venue, grassroots atmosphere
- **Price:** ₱ (budget-friendly)
- **Rating:** 4.0/5

### Elephant Bar — Makati

One of Makati's more established sports bars, Elephant Bar caters to the international crowd in a neighbourhood with no shortage of Irish pubs and expat watering holes. The football coverage is strong — Premier League and Champions League are reliably on — and it's a reasonable option when you want a quieter setting than the BGC venues on a big match night.

- **Best for:** Premier League, Champions League, rugby
- **Location:** Makati, near Ayala Avenue
- **Vibe:** International expat bar, relaxed
- **Price:** ₱₱ (mid-range)
- **Rating:** 4.0/5

### Tipple & Slaw — BGC

A newer entrant to the BGC scene that's quickly developed a following for American sport. NFL Sundays draw a strong crowd — the American expat community in Manila is large and vocal, and Tipple & Slaw has positioned itself as the NFL destination in the city. NBA and college basketball also feature prominently. Less focused on football than Draft or Handlebar, but the best in BGC for American sports specifically.

- **Best for:** NFL, NBA, college basketball
- **Location:** BGC, Taguig
- **Vibe:** American-leaning, loud on game nights
- **Price:** ₱₱ (mid-range)
- **Rating:** 4.1/5

## Practical Tips for Watching Sport in Manila

- **Book or arrive early for NBA play-offs and Finals.** Manila's basketball culture is not casual — venues fill up significantly for big games.
- **Check timings carefully.** Manila's UTC+8 timezone means NBA games cluster in morning viewing windows. Confirm start times locally before planning your night.
- **BGC for reliability, Makati for variety, QC for local atmosphere.** Choose your base depending on what kind of experience you want.
- **Call ahead for unusual fixtures.** Most BGC bars show Premier League as standard, but for NFL play-offs, UFC PPVs, or niche leagues, confirm in advance.
- **Grab sits for EPL weekends.** Saturday 11pm kick-offs are peak social time in Manila. The best venues fill up — particularly for Liverpool, Man Utd, and Arsenal games, which have enormous Filipino fan bases.
