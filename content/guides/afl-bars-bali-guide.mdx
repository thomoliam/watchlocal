Bali is the default overseas destination for Australian travellers, and AFL fans are no exception. Every year, tens of thousands of Australians visit the island during the AFL season (March to September), and many want to catch their team's games while they are away. The good news is that several Bali venues cater specifically to Aussie sports fans, with reliable Fox Sports feeds and a crowd that knows the difference between a mark and a handball.

This guide covers every venue in Bali that regularly shows AFL, with a focus on the two main expat and tourist hubs: Canggu and Seminyak.

## AEST to Bali Time Conversion

Bali operates on WITA (Central Indonesian Time), which is UTC+8 — the same timezone as Perth and Singapore, and two hours behind Sydney during the AFL season (AEST, UTC+10).

| AEST (Melbourne/Sydney) | WITA (Bali) | Typical AFL Slot |
|--------------------------|-------------|------------------|
| 1:45 p.m. (Sat) | 11:45 a.m. (Sat) | Saturday afternoon game |
| 4:35 p.m. (Sat) | 2:35 p.m. (Sat) | Saturday twilight |
| 7:25 p.m. (Sat) | 5:25 p.m. (Sat) | Saturday night game |
| 1:10 p.m. (Sun) | 11:10 a.m. (Sun) | Sunday early game |
| 3:20 p.m. (Sun) | 1:20 p.m. (Sun) | Sunday afternoon game |
| 7:30 p.m. (Fri) | 5:30 p.m. (Fri) | Friday night football |

**Key takeaway:** AFL in Bali is a daytime and early-evening affair. You will never need to wake up at an unreasonable hour or stay out past midnight. Friday night football kicks off at 5:30 p.m. — perfect for a sunset session.

## Seminyak

### Nirvana Sports Bar (Best Overall)

[Nirvana Sports Bar](/venues/nirvana-seminyak-bali) is the best venue for AFL in all of Bali. Run by Australians, the bar has a direct Fox Sports satellite feed that guarantees every AFL match in broadcast quality. The crowd on a Saturday afternoon during the season is almost entirely Australian, and the atmosphere during finals is electric.

**What makes it special:**

- 15 screens with dedicated Fox Footy feed
- Opens early on match days — kitchen fires up by 11 a.m. for the first bounce
- Full Aussie pub menu: chicken parma, fish and chips, meat pies
- AFL tipping competitions and promotions throughout the season
- Also shows NRL, cricket, and F1 — so your non-footy mates are covered

**Best for:** AFL, NRL, cricket, A-League, Wallabies

**Price range:** Mid-range. Bintang from 40,000 IDR, parma from 130,000 IDR.

**Rating:** 4.6 / 5

### Red Ruby

Red Ruby in Seminyak is a smaller, newer venue that has built a following among Aussie expats. It runs AFL on its main screen every weekend and offers drink specials during Melbourne and Collingwood matches (the two clubs with the strongest following in Bali).

**What makes it special:**

- Dedicated AFL audio on the main screen
- Club-specific promotions (wear your team colours for a discount)
- Smaller and more intimate than Nirvana — easy to meet fellow fans
- Good cocktail list for partners who are not watching the footy

**Best for:** AFL, Big Bash cricket

**Price range:** Mid-range. Beers from 45,000 IDR.

**Rating:** 4.2 / 5

## Canggu

Canggu is the digital-nomad capital of Bali, and its sports bars cater to a younger, international crowd. AFL coverage is less guaranteed here than in Seminyak, but the following venues are reliable.

### Old Man's

[Old Man's](/venues/old-mans-canggu-bali) is Canggu's most famous bar — a sprawling, open-air venue right on Batu Bolong Beach. While it is best known for sunset sessions and DJs, Old Man's shows AFL on weekends on its secondary screens. The atmosphere is laid-back and social, with Aussies making up a large portion of the crowd.

**What makes it special:**

- Beachfront location — watch the footy with an ocean view
- Large capacity, rarely turns people away
- Attracts a young Aussie crowd, so there is always someone to watch with
- Sunset and footy is a hard combination to beat

**Caveat:** Old Man's prioritises whatever the majority wants to watch. If there is a big EPL match on at the same time, the main screen may switch. Arrive early and stake your claim.

**Best for:** AFL (secondary screens), casual viewing

**Price range:** Mid-range. Bintang from 45,000 IDR.

**Rating:** 4.3 / 5

### Sand Bar Canggu

[Sand Bar](/venues/sand-bar-canggu-bali) is a dedicated sports bar in Canggu that covers a wide range of sports. It has reliable AFL coverage on weekends and a pool table area for between-quarter entertainment.

**What makes it special:**

- Multiple screens with independent feeds — AFL can always run alongside other sports
- Pool tables and beer pong
- Strong happy hour deals in the afternoon
- More sports-focused than Old Man's — less chance of losing the screen to music

**Best for:** AFL, NRL, EPL, UFC

**Price range:** Budget-friendly. Beers from 35,000 IDR during happy hour.

**Rating:** 4.1 / 5

### Deus Ex Machina

Deus in Canggu is a motorcycle-themed cafe-bar with a strong Aussie following. It shows AFL intermittently (not every match), but on big occasions — Anzac Day match, Brownlow Medal, Grand Final — it creates a proper event atmosphere.

**Best for:** Grand Final day, Anzac Day match, Brownlow Medal night

**Rating:** 4.0 / 5

## AFL Grand Final in Bali

The AFL Grand Final (last Saturday in September) is the biggest day on the Aussie sports calendar, and Bali goes all-in. The Grand Final bounces at 2:30 p.m. AEST, which is 12:30 p.m. in Bali — perfect for a long, boozy afternoon.

**What to expect:**

- Nirvana in Seminyak hosts a ticketed Grand Final party with a BBQ, drink packages, and big-screen projection
- Old Man's runs an outdoor Grand Final screening on the beach
- Every Aussie-friendly bar on the island will have it on — you will not struggle to find a screen
- Book your seat at Nirvana at least a week in advance; it sells out every year

**Pro tip:** If you are in Bali for Grand Final weekend, book your accommodation in Seminyak. The walk from Nirvana back to your hotel is much easier than a late-night Grab from Canggu.

## Practical Tips

### Which Venues Have Fox Footy?

Nirvana is the only venue with a guaranteed Fox Footy satellite feed. Other bars rely on streaming or shared satellite packages that may not include every Fox Footy channel. For marquee matches (Friday night, Saturday twilight, Sunday afternoon), most bars will have coverage. For Thursday-night or early-round matches, Nirvana is your safest bet.

### Wearing Team Colours

Wearing your club jumper to the bar is not just accepted — it is expected. You will spot Collingwood, Melbourne, Richmond, and Essendon guernseys everywhere. It is a great conversation starter and the fastest way to find fellow supporters.

### Food and Drink

Bali sports bars serve a mix of Aussie pub classics and Indonesian food. A chicken parma at Nirvana will set you back about 130,000 IDR (roughly 13 AUD), and a Bintang is around 40,000–50,000 IDR (4–5 AUD). That is roughly half what you would pay at your local RSL.

### Getting Between Venues

Canggu and Seminyak are about 20 minutes apart by scooter or Grab. If you are planning a full day of footy, pick one area and stick with it. Seminyak (Nirvana) is the better choice for dedicated AFL viewing; Canggu (Old Man's, Sand Bar) is better if you want a mix of sport and beach.
