# Where to Watch Premier League in Bangkok (2026 Guide)

Bangkok is one of the best cities in the world to watch Premier League football as an expat or traveller. The city's enormous international community, long-established Irish pub culture, and convenient time zone alignment mean that watching EPL in Bangkok is genuinely easy — and genuinely fun.

## What Time Does Premier League Kick Off in Bangkok?

Thailand is on ICT (Indochina Time), UTC+7 — seven hours ahead of the UK (BST in summer, GMT in winter).

| UK Kick-Off | Bangkok Time |
|---|---|
| Saturday 12:30 PM | Saturday 7:30 PM |
| Saturday 3:00 PM | Saturday 10:00 PM |
| Saturday 5:30 PM | Saturday 12:30 AM (Sunday) |
| Sunday 2:00 PM | Sunday 9:00 PM |
| Sunday 4:30 PM | Sunday 11:30 PM |
| Midweek 7:45 PM | Midweek 2:45 AM |

The Saturday lunchtime game is the sweet spot in Bangkok — prime evening viewing with a cold Chang. Later games push into late night territory, but Bangkok's sports bars are used to this and stay open accordingly.

## Best Areas for Premier League Bars in Bangkok

### Sukhumvit

The main expat corridor. Soi 4 (Nana), Soi 11, Soi 13, and Asok are the highest density areas for sports bars. The Sportsman on Sukhumvit Soi 13 is one of Bangkok's most famous — 34 screens, pool tables, open late.

### Silom

More of a local and tourist mix. Good options around Patpong and Silom Road. Slightly more relaxed atmosphere than Sukhumvit.

### Thonglor/Ekkamai

The upmarket Bangkok expat neighbourhood. Sports bars here tend to be more restaurant-bar hybrids — good food, good screens, pricier. Good for a big match occasion.

### Ari/Phrom Phong

Quieter, residential expat areas. Fewer sports bars but some hidden gems favoured by regulars who prefer a local pub feel.

## Broadcasting: Who Shows EPL in Thailand?

The Premier League is broadcast in Thailand via **TrueVisions** and **beIN Sports** (through the TrueVisions package). Sports bars in Bangkok showing EPL will typically have a TrueVisions subscription.

Free-to-air: PPTV holds rights to selected matches — hotels and bars without a sports package may still have access to some games this way.

## Tips for Watching EPL in Bangkok

- **Arrive early for big games.** Popular bars on Sukhumvit fill up for major Premier League weekends. Arrive 30 minutes before kick-off.
- **Happy hour runs early.** Most Bangkok sports bars do happy hour until 7-8pm — aligns perfectly with the Saturday lunchtime kick-off.
- **Fan groups exist.** Arsenal, Liverpool, Manchester United, and Chelsea all have organised supporter groups in Bangkok. Search Facebook for "[club name] Bangkok" to find the right bar.
- **Midweek games are late.** The 7:45pm UK kick-off is 2:45am in Bangkok. Some bars stay open for it — call ahead.
- **Check for Champions League.** Confirm the bar has beIN Sports if you want UCL coverage alongside EPL.

## The Supporter Bar Scene

Bangkok has a well-developed supporter club culture. Several bars have become the unofficial home for major clubs:
- Liverpool and Arsenal fans cluster at established venues on Sukhumvit
- Chelsea and Manchester United have dedicated supporter club meeting points
- Smaller clubs (Everton, Spurs) have WhatsApp groups that circulate the right venue each week
