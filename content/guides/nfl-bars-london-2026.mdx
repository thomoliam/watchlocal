London has one of the biggest NFL fanbases outside North America. The league has played regular-season games at Tottenham Hotspur Stadium and Wembley since 2007, and the UK fanbase has grown from a niche following to a mainstream audience. Sky Sports and the NFL's own Game Pass carry every game, and a growing number of London pubs and bars cater specifically to American football fans.

Watching NFL in London does require some commitment. The 1 p.m. ET Sunday slate kicks off at 6 p.m. GMT, which is manageable. But the late games run past midnight, and Monday Night Football does not start until 1:15 a.m. on Tuesday — a serious ask on a work night. This guide covers the best venues for every slot.

## NFL Game Times in London (GMT)

| US Time (ET) | London (GMT) | Slot |
|-------------|-------------|------|
| 9:30 a.m. Sun | 2:30 p.m. Sun | London/International Game |
| 1:00 p.m. Sun | 6:00 p.m. Sun | Early Sunday slate |
| 4:05 / 4:25 p.m. Sun | 9:05 / 9:25 p.m. Sun | Late Sunday slate |
| 8:20 p.m. Sun | 1:20 a.m. Mon | Sunday Night Football |
| 8:15 p.m. Mon | 1:15 a.m. Tue | Monday Night Football |
| 8:15 p.m. Thu | 1:15 a.m. Fri | Thursday Night Football |

**Key takeaway:** The early and late Sunday slates are the sweet spot. You can watch a full afternoon of football from 6 p.m. to 11:30 p.m., then head home at a reasonable hour. Sunday Night Football and Monday Night Football are for the truly dedicated (or those who work from home).

**During BST (clocks forward in late March):** Add one hour to all the times above. The early Sunday slate kicks off at 7 p.m. BST.

## The Five Best NFL Bars in London

### 1. Bodean's BBQ — Soho

[Bodean's](/venues/bodeans-soho-london) on Poland Street in Soho is the spiritual home of NFL in London. The original Kansas City-style BBQ restaurant has been screening American football since long before the NFL London Games started, and it remains the first choice for serious fans.

**What makes it special:**

- Screens every Sunday slate game with dedicated NFL audio
- Authentic Kansas City BBQ — pulled pork, brisket, burnt ends, ribs
- Reserved seating available for the Sunday session (book by Thursday)
- Stays open late for Sunday Night Football on big weeks
- NFL memorabilia and jerseys cover the walls
- Strong craft beer selection with American imports

**Best for:** The full Sunday session (early slate through late slate)

**Price range:** Mid-range ($$). Mains from 14 GBP, pints from 6 GBP.

**Rating:** 4.5 / 5

### 2. The Hippodrome Casino — Leicester Square

[The Hippodrome](/venues/hippodrome-london) in Leicester Square hosts a weekly NFL screening event during the season. The venue dedicates a floor to American football on Sundays, with multiple screens, drink deals, and a buzzing atmosphere.

**What makes it special:**

- Dedicated NFL floor with big-screen projection
- Free entry (you just need to register)
- RedZone on the main screen with individual games on surrounding TVs
- Late-night licence — stays open for Sunday Night Football and beyond
- Central London location, easy to reach from anywhere

**Best for:** RedZone viewing, late-night games, group outings

**Price range:** Mid-range ($$). Drinks from 6 GBP.

**Rating:** 4.4 / 5

### 3. Belushi's — London Bridge

[Belushi's](/venues/belushis-london-bridge) at London Bridge is part of the St Christopher's hostel chain and has become a reliable NFL haunt. The bar screens every Sunday game and attracts a young, international crowd. It is one of the few London venues that consistently stays open for Monday Night Football.

**What makes it special:**

- Always open for Thursday Night, Sunday Night, and Monday Night Football
- Large venue with multiple screens and a late-night licence
- Affordable by London standards — regular drink promotions
- Popular with American tourists and exchange students
- Also screens NBA, MLB, and college football on request

**Best for:** Late-night games (SNF, MNF, TNF), budget-friendly viewing

**Price range:** Budget ($). Pints from 5 GBP, regular 2-for-1 deals.

**Rating:** 4.2 / 5

### 4. The Admiralty — Trafalgar Square

[The Admiralty](/venues/the-admiralty-london) is a Greene King pub near Trafalgar Square that has built a loyal NFL following. The upstairs room is dedicated to American football on Sundays, with multiple screens and booth seating.

**What makes it special:**

- Upstairs room dedicated to NFL on Sundays — no competition for the screens
- Proper pub food: burgers, wings, fish and chips
- Central location near Charing Cross station
- Quieter and more focused than the mega-venues
- Real ale selection alongside the standard lagers

**Best for:** Fans who prefer a pub atmosphere over a party atmosphere

**Price range:** Mid-range ($$). Pints from 6.50 GBP.

**Rating:** 4.3 / 5

### 5. Freight Island (at Vinegar Yard) — London Bridge

Vinegar Yard near London Bridge is an open-air food market that hosts NFL screening events on Sundays during the season. The setup varies week to week, but typically includes a large outdoor screen, street food vendors, and a bar.

**What makes it special:**

- Outdoor screening experience — unique in London
- Street food from multiple vendors
- Casual, festival-like atmosphere
- Popular with groups and first-time NFL viewers
- Dog-friendly

**Caveat:** Outdoor screenings are weather-dependent. Check their social media for weekly confirmation.

**Best for:** Casual fans, groups, the early Sunday slate in good weather

**Price range:** Mid-range ($$). Food from 8 GBP, drinks from 5.50 GBP.

**Rating:** 4.1 / 5

## NFL London Games

The NFL plays two to four regular-season games in London each year, typically in October. Games are held at Tottenham Hotspur Stadium (which has a purpose-built NFL-regulation pitch under its football surface) and occasionally at Wembley Stadium.

### Key Information

- **Kickoff time:** Usually 2:30 p.m. GMT (9:30 a.m. ET)
- **Tickets:** Go on sale via Ticketmaster in the spring. General sale sells out in minutes for marquee matchups. Join the NFL UK mailing list for presale access.
- **Atmosphere:** London NFL games are a genuine event. Tailgating is limited (no car parks), but the NFL Fan Experience in Regent Street (the Thursday before game day) and Tottenham's fan zone offer pre-game entertainment.
- **Jersey rule:** Wear any team's jersey. London games attract fans of all 32 teams, and the crowd is a colourful mix.
- **Getting there:** Tottenham Hotspur Stadium is served by White Hart Lane (Overground) and Tottenham Hale (Victoria line). Wembley is on the Metropolitan and Jubilee lines.

### Watching the London Game at a Bar

If you cannot get tickets, every NFL bar in this guide will screen the London game. Bodean's and The Hippodrome both host special London Game events with early opening and themed menus.

## Super Bowl in London

The Super Bowl kicks off at approximately 11:30 p.m. GMT (6:30 p.m. ET) on the first Sunday in February. The game runs until around 3 a.m.

**Where to watch:**

- **Bodean's** hosts a ticketed Super Bowl party with a BBQ buffet and drink packages. Tickets sell out weeks in advance.
- **The Hippodrome** opens its NFL floor for a free Super Bowl event with screenings and competitions.
- **Belushi's** runs a Super Bowl all-nighter with themed cocktails and halftime entertainment.
- Many pubs and bars across London screen the Super Bowl even if they do not normally show NFL — it has become a mainstream event.

**Pro tip:** The Monday after the Super Bowl is not a UK bank holiday. Plan your next day accordingly.

## Practical Tips for NFL Fans in London

### Arriving in Kit

Wearing an NFL jersey in London is completely normal, especially on Sundays during the season and around London Game weekends. You will get nods of recognition and the occasional "Go [team]!" from fellow fans.

### Finding Fellow Fans

The UK has active fan communities for most NFL teams. Search Facebook for "[Team Name] UK" to find supporters groups that meet regularly at specific pubs. The Jacksonville Jaguars, who have played in London annually since 2013, have one of the largest UK fanbases.

### Food and Drink

London NFL bars serve a mix of American-style food (wings, ribs, burgers) and British pub classics. Portion sizes are smaller than US equivalents, and beer is more expensive (expect 5.50–7 GBP per pint). Bodean's is the closest you will get to an authentic American BBQ experience.

### RedZone vs. Individual Games

Most London NFL bars default to RedZone on the main screen during the early and late Sunday slates, with individual games on surrounding TVs. If you want to follow a specific game, arrive early and ask the bar staff to put it on a nearby screen. At larger venues like The Hippodrome, you can usually find every game somewhere in the room.

### The Late-Night Question

Sunday Night Football (1:20 a.m. Monday) and Monday Night Football (1:15 a.m. Tuesday) are the biggest challenges for London-based NFL fans. Only Belushi's and The Hippodrome reliably stay open for these slots. If your team plays on Monday night, you may want to stream it at home via Game Pass rather than venture out.
