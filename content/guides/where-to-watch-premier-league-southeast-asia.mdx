The Premier League is the most-watched football league in the world, and Southeast Asia is one of its biggest markets. Millions of fans across the region tune in every weekend, and the timezone is close to ideal — most Saturday 3 p.m. GMT kickoffs land between 8 p.m. and 11 p.m. local time, perfect for an evening out at a sports bar.

This guide covers the five best cities in Southeast Asia for watching the Premier League, with venue recommendations, kickoff time tables, and broadcaster information for each country.

## Kickoff Times by Timezone

Premier League matches are scheduled around UK time (GMT in winter, BST in summer). Here is how those translate across Southeast Asia:

| UK Time (GMT) | Bangkok (ICT) | Bali (WITA) | Singapore (SGT) | HCMC (ICT) | KL (MYT) |
|---------------|--------------|-------------|-----------------|------------|----------|
| 12:30 p.m. | 7:30 p.m. | 8:30 p.m. | 8:30 p.m. | 7:30 p.m. | 8:30 p.m. |
| 3:00 p.m. | 10:00 p.m. | 11:00 p.m. | 11:00 p.m. | 10:00 p.m. | 11:00 p.m. |
| 5:30 p.m. | 12:30 a.m.+1 | 1:30 a.m.+1 | 1:30 a.m.+1 | 12:30 a.m.+1 | 1:30 a.m.+1 |
| 8:00 p.m. | 3:00 a.m.+1 | 4:00 a.m.+1 | 4:00 a.m.+1 | 3:00 a.m.+1 | 4:00 a.m.+1 |

**Key takeaway:** The early and mid-afternoon kickoffs are the sweet spot. Late kickoffs (the Monday night 8 p.m. slot) fall in the middle of the night and are harder to catch live.

Most sports bars in the region carry the official broadcast via satellite or licensed IPTV. If a bar is using unofficial streams, you will notice buffering and lower quality — worth keeping in mind when choosing a venue.

## Bangkok, Thailand

Bangkok has the deepest sports bar scene in Southeast Asia, with more than a dozen venues along Sukhumvit Road alone. The city is a magnet for British, Australian, and Kiwi expats, all of whom take their football seriously.

### Best venue: The Sportsman

[The Sportsman](/venues/the-sportsman-bangkok) on Soi 13 is the best sports bar in Bangkok by a wide margin. Two floors, 39 screens, separate audio zones, and a kitchen that serves full English breakfasts from opening. It shows every Premier League match, and the early kickoffs draw serious crowds.

### Also recommended

- [The Robin Hood](/venues/the-robin-hood-bangkok) — proper British pub atmosphere, home to several supporters groups
- [Stumble Inn](/venues/stumble-inn-bangkok) — massive ground floor, great for groups, cheap beer

### Atmosphere

Bangkok bars get loud for the big matches. Liverpool vs. Manchester City or a North London derby will pack out The Sportsman by 9 p.m. Expect singing, chanting, and a few spilled pints. Quieter pubs like The Robin Hood suit fans who want to hear the commentary.

## Bali, Indonesia

Bali's sports bar scene is concentrated in two areas: Seminyak and Canggu. The island draws a mixed crowd of Australian surfers, digital nomads, and European holidaymakers, so there is demand for a wide range of leagues. Premier League matches typically screen at 8:30 p.m. or 11 p.m. on Saturday evenings — perfectly timed for a night out.

### Best venue: Nirvana Sports Bar

[Nirvana Sports Bar](/venues/nirvana-seminyak-bali) in Seminyak is the most reliable venue for Premier League in Bali. It has 15 screens, a dedicated football area, and a strong satellite feed. The crowd is a mix of expats and travellers, and the atmosphere picks up noticeably for top-six clashes.

### Also recommended

- [Old Man's](/venues/old-mans-canggu-bali) — Canggu's iconic beach bar, screens EPL on weekends
- [Sand Bar](/venues/sand-bar-canggu-bali) — lively Canggu venue with pool tables

### Atmosphere

Bali bars are more relaxed than Bangkok's pub-style venues. Expect a younger, international crowd in flip-flops and tank tops. The vibe is social first, sport second — but the TVs are always on.

## Singapore

Singapore has a mature sports bar scene with higher prices but excellent production quality. Many venues have private rooms with dedicated screens that can be booked for groups.

### Best venue: Muddy Murphy's

Muddy Murphy's is a long-standing Irish pub in the Orchard area that has become a go-to for EPL fans. The bar opens early on weekends for the lunchtime kickoff (8:30 p.m. SGT for the 12:30 GMT slot) and stays busy through the evening.

### Also recommended

- Harry's Bar — multiple locations, solid screens, and a reliable satellite feed
- The Penny Black — Victorian-themed pub near Boat Quay, popular with City and United fans

### Atmosphere

Singapore bars are more controlled than Bangkok or Bali. There is less singing and more focused viewing. The trade-off is better sound quality, cleaner venues, and faster service. Pint prices start at around 12–15 SGD.

## Ho Chi Minh City, Vietnam

HCMC (Saigon) is rapidly growing as an expat hub, and the sports bar scene has expanded along with it. The city shares Bangkok's timezone (ICT, UTC+7), so kickoff times are identical. Venues tend to cluster around District 1 (the backpacker and expat centre) and Thao Dien in District 2.

### Best venue: Phatty's

Phatty's in District 1 is a rooftop sports bar with a strong following among British and Irish expats. The venue has multiple screens, a well-stocked bar, and serves food until late. Premier League matchdays draw a loyal crowd.

### Also recommended

- Murphy's Irish Pub — District 1, reliable EPL coverage, good Guinness
- Saigon Sports Club — District 2, popular with families and the Thao Dien expat crowd

### Atmosphere

HCMC bars have a warm, welcoming feel. The expat community is tight-knit, and newcomers are quickly absorbed. Beer is cheap — 30,000–50,000 VND (roughly 1.20–2 USD) for a draught at most sports bars.

## Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia

KL shares Singapore's timezone (MYT, UTC+8) and has a growing number of sports-focused venues. Astro SuperSport carries all 380 Premier League matches, so any bar with an Astro subscription can show the games.

### Best venue: The Strata Bar

The Strata Bar in KLCC is a modern sports bar with large screens and a dedicated football area. The venue is popular with both locals and expats and benefits from its central location near the Petronas Towers.

### Also recommended

- SOULed OUT — Desa Sri Hartamas, large venue with outdoor area and screens
- Sid's Pub — Bangsar, British-style pub with strong EPL coverage

### Atmosphere

KL's sports bar scene sits between Singapore's polished venues and Bangkok's raucous pubs. Prices are reasonable (draught beer from 15–20 MYR), and the mix of Malay, Chinese, Indian, and expat fans makes for a diverse atmosphere.

## Tips for Watching EPL in Southeast Asia

### Arrive Early

For headline matches (top-six clashes, derbies, final day), arrive at least 30 minutes before kickoff to secure a good seat. Venues like The Sportsman in Bangkok can fill up fast.

### Check the Broadcast

Most bars use official broadcasters, but some rely on unofficial IPTV streams that can buffer or drop during peak demand. If picture quality matters to you, ask the bar staff what feed they use.

### Join a Supporters Group

Many cities have official supporters groups for major EPL clubs. These groups often have reserved areas at specific bars and organise travel to away days in the region. Facebook is the best place to find them — search for "[Club Name] Bangkok" or "[Club Name] Bali."

### Consider the Late Kickoff

The 5:30 p.m. GMT kickoff (12:30 a.m. local in Bangkok/HCMC, 1:30 a.m. in Bali/Singapore/KL) is manageable if you plan ahead. Some bars close at midnight, so check closing times before committing.
