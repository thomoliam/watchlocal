The Super Bowl is the most-watched single sporting event in the world, with an audience that extends far beyond the United States. For fans living or travelling abroad, watching the Super Bowl live means either a very late night or a very early morning — and finding the right venue makes all the difference.

This guide covers how to find a Super Bowl venue abroad, understand local kickoff times, and make the most of the experience.

## Super Bowl Kickoff Times Around the World

The Super Bowl typically kicks off between **6:30pm and 6:40pm EST** (Eastern Standard Time), usually on the second Sunday of February. This translates to:

| Location | Local Time | Day |
|----------|-----------|-----|
| London (GMT) | 11:30pm | Sunday night |
| Paris / Amsterdam / Berlin (CET) | 00:30am | Monday morning |
| Dubai (GST) | 02:30am | Monday morning |
| Bangkok (ICT) | 06:30am | Monday morning |
| Singapore / Kuala Lumpur (SGT) | 07:30am | Monday morning |
| Sydney (AEDT) | 10:30am | Monday morning |
| Auckland (NZDT) | 12:30pm | Monday afternoon |

**The game itself runs approximately 4–5 hours** including halftime. Factor that into your plan: if kickoff is at 11:30pm in London, you're likely leaving the venue around 3:30am.

## Super Bowl Venues Worth Seeking Out

### London

London is one of the better cities for Super Bowl watching outside the US. The NFL has a strong following in London (several regular-season games are played at Wembley and Tottenham Hotspur Stadium), which means genuine American football fans in the crowd rather than casual onlookers.

Sports bars around Soho, the City, and Shoreditch host Super Bowl nights with varying levels of production. The best venues lay on food, run prop-bet competitions, and screen the halftime show in full. Book well ahead for anything in Zone 1.

### Bangkok

Bangkok is uniquely placed for the Super Bowl — the 6:30am Monday kickoff is late enough to have slept, early enough to get to work (eventually). Several sports bars on Sukhumvit open for a Super Bowl breakfast, with American food laid on and the game on every screen.

This is becoming a tradition for Bangkok's expat community — the Monday morning Super Bowl has a distinctly festive feel.

### Sydney

Sydney timing (10:30am Monday) is genuinely comfortable — which is one reason Australia has developed a strong NFL following. Sports bars in the CBD and inner-west open early for the game, and the Monday morning timing makes it accessible in a way it simply isn't in European time zones.

### Singapore

Singapore's 7:30am kickoff is manageable. Several sports bars in Clarke Quay and Boat Quay offer Super Bowl breakfasts on Monday morning — a surprisingly popular event given the quality of the sports bars and the critical mass of American expats in the city.

## What to Look for in a Super Bowl Venue

**Screen size.** The Super Bowl is a spectacle — the commercials, the halftime show, and the game itself all deserve a large screen. Don't settle for a venue where the big game is playing on a 42-inch TV in the corner.

**Sound quality.** The halftime show is part of the Super Bowl experience. A venue that mutes the music or cuts away to a presenter discussion during the break misses the point.

**American-style food.** Wings, nachos, loaded fries — the Super Bowl is one game where the food at the venue genuinely matters. Look for venues that run a dedicated Super Bowl food menu.

**A crowd.** The Super Bowl is best watched with others. A venue with 10 people in it at midnight doesn't have the energy the game deserves. Check in advance whether the bar is running an event or just screening the game quietly.

**Prop bets and games.** Many venues run Super Bowl bingo, squares pools, or MVP predictions. These enhance the experience considerably, especially for casual fans.

## Super Bowl Tips for Abroad

1. **Book early.** Venues that do Super Bowl properly (big screen, food, events) fill up. In London and Singapore, good venues sell out weeks in advance.

2. **Find where Americans gather.** In most major cities, the American expat community has designated venues for the Super Bowl. Local expat groups and Facebook communities will know exactly where to go.

3. **Know the game runs long.** With four-hour game time, long commercial breaks, and a halftime show, the Super Bowl typically doesn't finish until 5–6 hours after kickoff. Plan your next day accordingly.

4. **Don't worry if you don't follow the NFL.** The Super Bowl is one of the few sporting events enjoyable even without a rooting interest. The halftime show, the ads (if you can access American streams), and the event spectacle are entertaining regardless.

5. **Check if the venue shows the American broadcast.** US Super Bowl ads are legendary. Some venues specifically show the US broadcast feed — worth seeking out for the full experience.
